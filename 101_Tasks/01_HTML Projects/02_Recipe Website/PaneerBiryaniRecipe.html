Paneer Biryani

Description

Paneer Biryani is a flavorful vegetarian rice dish made with aromatic basmati rice, spiced yogurt-marinated paneer, fried onions, fresh herbs, saffron and whole spices. The rice and paneer are layered and slow-cooked using the traditional dum method, allowing the flavors and aroma to blend beautifully.

Preparation Time

  • Prep Time: 30 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 20 minutes
  • Total Time: Approximately 1 hour 5 minutes
  • Servings: 3

Note: The preparation time includes soaking and marinating

Cooking Time

20 minutes

  • Rice preparation: ~10-15 minutes
  • Dum cooking: ~15-20 minutes

The final dum stage can be done on the stovetop or in an oven.

Ingredients

For the Biryani

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 200-250 g paneer, cubed
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup fresh curd/yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 green chilli, chopped
  • 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
  • ½ tbsp mint leaves, chopped
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ¼ tsp red chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ tsp shahi jeera
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste

Whole Spices

  • 1-inch cinnamon stick
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 2-3 green cardamoms
  • ¼ tsp shahi jeera

For Garnishing

  • ¼ cup milk
  • 16-18 saffron strands
  • 1-2 tbsp ghee
  • Fried onions
  • Fresh coriander
  • Fresh mint

The ingredient proportions are adapted from a traditional dum-style paneer biryani recipe.

Instructions

Step 1 - Prepare the Rice

  1. Wash 1 cup basmati rice until the water becomes relatively clear.
  2. Soak the rice for about 30 minutes.
  3. Bring around 4 cups of water to a boil.
  4. Add cinnamon, bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, shahi jeera and salt.
  5. Add the soaked rice.
  6. Cook until the rice is approximately 75% cooked.
  7. Drain the rice and keep it aside.

Step 2 - Fry the Onions

  1. Thinly slice the onion.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan.
  3. Fry the onions over medium-low heat until golden brown.
  4. Remove and place them on a paper towel.
  5. Keep the fried onions aside for layering.

Step 3 - Marinate the Paneer

  1. Add curd to a bowl and whisk until smooth.
  2. Add ginger paste, garlic paste and green chilli.
  3. Add turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala and coriander powder.
  4. Add lemon juice and salt.
  5. Add chopped coriander and mint.
  6. Add paneer cubes and some fried onions.
  7. Mix gently.
  8. Cover and marinate for approximately 30 minutes.

Step 4 - Prepare Saffron Milk

  1. Warm ¼ cup milk.
  2. Add saffron strands.
  3. Mix and allow the saffron to infuse into the milk.

Step 5 - Layer the Biryani

  1. Add some of the leftover onion-frying oil or ghee to a heavy-bottomed pot.
  2. Add the marinated paneer.
  3. Spread the partially cooked rice over the paneer.
  4. Add the remaining fried onions.
  5. Sprinkle coriander and mint.
  6. Pour the saffron milk over the rice.
  7. Add 1-2 tablespoons of ghee.

Step 6 - Dum Cook

  1. Cover the pot tightly with a lid.
  2. Cook on medium heat for about 5 minutes.
  3. Reduce the heat to low.
  4. Continue cooking for approximately 12-15 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat and let the biryani rest for a few minutes.
  6. Gently fluff the layers before serving.

Serve hot with raita, plain curd, salad or salan.
