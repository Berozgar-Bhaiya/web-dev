Chocolate Cake

Description

Chocolate Cake is a rich, soft and moist dessert made with cocoa powder, flour, sugar, milk and chocolate. This version has a deep chocolate flavor and a smooth chocolate ganache topping, making it ideal for birthdays, celebrations or simply as a dessert.

Preparation Time

  • Prep Time: 30 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 30-35 minutes
  • Cooling Time: 30 minutes
  • Total Time: Approximately 1 hour 25 minutes
  • Servings: 8

Cooking Time

30-35 minutes

Bake the cake at 180°C (350°F) until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

Ingredients

For the Chocolate Cake

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup milk
  • ½ cup hot water or hot coffee

For Chocolate Ganache

  • 150 g dark or semi-sweet chocolate
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon butter, optional

For Decoration

  • Chocolate shavings
  • Chocolate chips
  • Cocoa powder, optional

Instructions

Step 1 - Prepare the Batter

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Grease an 8-inch cake pan and line the bottom with parchment paper.
  3. In a large bowl, combine flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda and salt.
  4. In another bowl, whisk together sugar, eggs, oil and vanilla extract.
  5. Add the milk and mix well.
  6. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients.
  7. Mix until just combined.
  8. Slowly add the hot water or coffee and mix until you get a smooth, relatively thin batter.

Step 2 - Bake the Cake

  1. Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
  2. Gently tap the pan to remove large air bubbles.
  3. Bake for 30-35 minutes.
  4. Insert a toothpick into the center.
  5. If it comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, the cake is ready.
  6. Allow the cake to cool in the pan for about 10 minutes.
  7. Transfer it to a cooling rack and let it cool completely.

Step 3 - Prepare the Chocolate Ganache

  1. Chop the chocolate into small pieces.
  2. Heat the cream until it is hot but not boiling.
  3. Pour the hot cream over the chocolate.
  4. Let it sit for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Stir until completely smooth.
  6. Add butter if you want a smoother and shinier ganache.
  7. Allow the ganache to cool slightly until it reaches a spreadable consistency.

Step 4 - Assemble the Cake

  1. Place the cooled cake on a serving plate.
  2. Spread the chocolate ganache evenly over the top.
  3. Let some ganache drip naturally down the sides.
  4. Add chocolate shavings or chocolate chips on top.
  5. Slice and serve.
